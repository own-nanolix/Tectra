## Type

- **Urbanist** (Google Font, open licence), weights **Light 300, Regular 400, Medium 500**. It is the real brand
  font and is free, so it is loaded directly through `next/font/google`. No secondary typeface is shown; the site
  uses Urbanist for everything and shows its weights as the "secondary" voice (Medium for labels and card titles).
- Scale seen at 1600 px wide: display quotes about 88 px Light, line-height about 1.25; section headings about
  88 px Light centred; app hero "Smart Construction" about 64 px Light, line-height about 1.05; card figures
  ("76%") about 40 px Light; quote body 30 to 36 px Light; card titles 20 to 22 px Medium; body 20 px Light or
  Regular; chips and labels 18 to 20 px Regular; axis and captions 12 to 14 px Regular; footer heads 11 px Medium
  upper case.
- Letter-spacing: slightly tight on display sizes (about -0.01em), normal elsewhere. Case: sentence case for body
  and cards, Title Case for section headings, upper case only for footer column heads.
- Habits: one phrase per heading or quote is set in Tectra Blue; quotes open with a straight double quote and
  have no closing quote; numbers are Light and large; percent signs are full size.

## Gradients, textures, backgrounds

- The brand is almost flat. Gradients appear only as: bars that fade vertically from full colour to transparent;
  the area chart's white fill fading down; glass panels (white 12% to 22%); a soft lighter-to-deeper blue across
  the hero (`#5BA8F8` to `#4C9BF3`).
- The texture of the brand is **line drawing**: dark-blue outlines (trees as scalloped cloud shapes on thin
  trunks, fences, a pitched-roof building seen in isometric cut-away) over the blue field, or grey outlines on
  white with only the roofs filled blue.
- The isometric building: an H / L plan of long sheds with the walls open at the top, ridge roofs drawn as deep
  blue A-frames, vertical board lines on the walls, small windows and doors, white ring pins on the roof.

## Motion personality

Calm, measured, instrument-like: things are **drawn, measured and filled**, never bounced.

- `--ease-out: cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)` for entrances and fills (survey-line settle).
- `--ease-in-out: cubic-bezier(0.65, 0, 0.35, 1)` for moves between states (handles, tabs, steppers).
- `--ease-draw: cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1)` for line drawing and arcs.
- `--ease-snap: cubic-bezier(0.3, 1.3, 0.5, 1)` only for pins and dots landing (a small overshoot).
- Durations: `--dur-1: 140ms` (hover, press), `--dur-2: 280ms` (toggles, chips), `--dur-3: 520ms` (cards, reveals),
  `--dur-4: 900ms` (charts, gauges), `--dur-5: 1600ms` (logo and drawing draw-on). Stagger 60 ms.
- Signature moves: tick bars rise one by one left to right; range handles slide to their values; arcs sweep from
  the corner; pins drop and ring once; the two large circles of the mark slide together and fuse, then the small
  circle lands; drawings draw on by stroke.

## The brand idea (ours, from this case study only)

Tectra turns a building site into something you can read like an instrument: one calm blue field, a drawing of
the job, and a few measured figures with yellow marking what needs attention. The mark says the same thing in
three circles: separate pieces of a project fusing into one body, with the next piece on its way.
