# LAYOUTS: one line per page, one line per mockup

Direction for this project (from `rule/07-tectra.md`, section 2b):

- **Mockup staging:** exploded view: each object pulled apart into its layers along one axis.
- **Mockup backdrop:** pure white with coloured contact shadows.
- **Brand pages lean on:** an editorial column with margin notes and pull-out figures.
- **Dashboard option A is:** an icon rail with a bento of widgets; B to E are four other structures.
- **Landing pages lean on:** data-led sections built around live widgets.

Rules: where the case study shows a layout, the case study wins. Nothing repeats: no two lines below may be
built the same way. Builders follow their lines; reviewers check against them.
